# Pizza Neopolitana

## Ingredients
    • 500g flour - Tipo 00 Caputo Pizzeria
    • 325g cold water – 65% hydration
    • 15g salt – 3%
    • 0.75g dry yeast – 1.5g fresh yeast – 0.0015%

## Dough
    • put some water on the salt
    • put some water on the yeast to dissolve
    • put the dissolved yeast in the flour
    • add more water gradually
    • last step is to put the salt with the remaining water in
    • form a nice dough ball by rolling the dought on the table – check movies
    • let it rest for 1 hour at room temperatur

## dough balls
    • cut it in pieces of 200g
    • fold it 4 times and then do the mozzarella move – check movies
    • 24h in the fridge
    • 4h befor baking out of fridge

## Finish
    • get it with the spatula and some semola
    • put it in semola
    • make it round
    • push from center to the edges
    • turn around again from center to the edges
    • turn around again
    • remove all semola underneath and on the dough itself
    • rotate and pull with one hand and the other one rests
    • put on what you want
